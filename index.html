📖 How to Play

🎯 The Goal

Every case has a goal at the top of the board. Collect the tiles shown before you run out of Moves. Solve the case to unlock the next one.

👆 Making Matches

Tap a tile, then tap a tile next to it to swap them, or just swipe a tile toward its neighbor. Line up 3 or more of the same tile in a row or column to clear them. A swap only works if it makes a match.

⛓ Chain Reactions

When new tiles fall and make more matches, you get a chain. Each link in the chain is worth more points.

🪂 Glide Tiles (match 4)

Match 4 in a row and one of those tiles turns into a glide tile with a double-headed arrow on it. The arrow shows which way it sweeps: ↔️ clears the whole row it sits in, and ↕️ clears the whole column. Match 4 sideways to get ↔️, or 4 up and down to get ↕️. Then include the glide tile in any match to set it off, just like Jet gliding across the park.

💥 Tumble Tiles (match 5 or an L/T shape)

Match 5 in a row, or make an L or T shape, and you get a 💥 tile. Match it and it clears every tile around it, like one of Fox’s famous slips and falls.

🎭 Red’s Disguise

In cases where Red is on the board, a chain of 3 or more makes one whole kind of tile switch disguises and turn into something else.

🌰 Clue Tiles

The Pinecone Tree, the SWAT Badge and the Lone Pine Park Sign are clue tiles. In some cases they are the goal, and in others they just help you build matches.

⚠️ Case Twists

From Case 3 on, every case has its own twist, shown under the goal bar:
Fox’s slips swap two tiles. The two tiles glow so you can see what moved, and one becomes a glide tile.
🐾 Paw prints, 🚧 fences, the guard dog and 🪵 the dam cover squares. Match the tiles on them to clear them, and clear them all to win. Guard-dog squares need 2 matches.
Quills, 🚧 fences and the guard dog pin their tiles in place. You can’t swap them, but you can match them right where they sit.
Snatchers take one of your collected tiles every few moves. The number lights up red when that happens, so keep collecting!

⭐ Stars

Finish with more moves left to earn up to 3 stars. Replay any solved case to beat your best score.

🌲 Helpers

Shake the Tree shuffles the board. Clue Me In shows you a move that will work. If you ever run out of moves, the board shakes itself.

🗂 Team Dossier

Solve cases to unlock characters. Tap them on the HQ screen to read their fun facts.
